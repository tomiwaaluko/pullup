I want to build a web app for college students that lets them know of what events are happening on campus. Before we get into the tech stack, can we brainstorm what to add to this idea?

Got it ✅ — let’s flesh out the features and value of the app first, then we can worry about the tech stack later. Here are some angles to brainstorm around:

🔑 Core Features

Event Listings: Date, time, location, description, RSVP option.

Filters & Search: Filter by type (academic, sports, social, career fair, Greek life, club meetings, etc.), by organization, or by day/week.

Calendar Integration: Add to Google Calendar/Apple Calendar with one tap.

RSVP & Reminders: Push notifications or email reminders for events you RSVP to.

💡 Engagement Features

Personalized Feed: Recommend events based on major, interests, or organizations followed.

Club Pages: Each org/club can manage their events, post updates, and build a following.

Gamification: Points or badges for attending events, with possible rewards (merch, food vouchers, extra credit if partnered with faculty).

Social Sharing: Share events with friends or groups (link, QR code, or in-app messaging).

📍 Campus Integration

Interactive Map: Show event locations on a campus map, with walking directions.

Live Updates: Highlight ongoing events (“Happening Now”) and near-you events.

Check-In: QR code scan at event entrances to track attendance.

🛠 Admin/Org Features

Event Creation Dashboard: Easy form for student orgs to submit events.

Approval Workflow: Optional admin moderation before events go public.

Analytics: Show clubs how many people viewed, RSVP’d, and attended their events.

🔒 Practical Features

Authentication: Sign in with student email (e.g., @ucf.edu) so only students access the platform.

Accessibility: Dark mode, screen reader compatibility, easy-to-read layouts.

Cross-Platform: Mobile-first design (web + mobile app later).

🚀 Extra Ideas

“Free Food Alert” tag for events that provide food (always a hit).

Collaboration Mode: Multiple orgs hosting an event together show up under all their pages.

Polls/Feedback: Quick surveys after events for engagement.

Integration with SGA/Student Life: Official feed of events from the school calendar.

Perfect — let’s zoom in on long-term, standout features that could make your app feel like more than just another campus calendar. These go beyond the MVP and add that “wow” factor once you’ve got a user base:

🌟 Long-Term, Standout Features

1. Smart Personalization

AI-based recommendations: suggest events based on classes taken, clubs joined, or past attendance.

“For You” feed similar to TikTok/Spotify Discover Weekly for campus events.

2. Campus Social Layer

Event group chats: temporary chat rooms where attendees can connect before/after an event.

Friend tracking: see what events your friends RSVP’d to or are checked in at.

“Meet People Like You”: suggest events where students with the same major/interests go.

3. Real-Time Interactivity

Live polls during events (e.g., talent shows, trivia nights).

Live event coverage (photos, highlights, or updates) from orgs or attendees.

Heat maps showing where the most students are currently hanging out on campus.

4. Partnerships & Incentives

Loyalty rewards system: attend X number of events → earn free campus merch or dining points.

Integration with local businesses: discounts or freebies for event attendees.

Ticketing/paywall integration for concerts, sports, or special events.

5. Career & Academic Tie-Ins

Career center integration: automatically pull in career fairs, recruiter events, resume workshops.

Professor/department event posts: research talks, office hours, or guest speakers.

“Academic + Fun balance”: recommend a mix of academic/professional events and social ones.

6. Enhanced Campus Map Features

AR campus navigation: hold up your phone to get real-time arrows to event locations.

“Where’s the free food?” overlay on the map.

Accessibility routes for wheelchair-friendly paths.

7. Community Building

Alumni access: alumni can see/post events relevant to networking or mentorship.

Volunteer opportunities and service events listed alongside social ones.

Club discovery engine: join new orgs based on events you attend.

8. Integrations with Student Life Systems

Sync with official university calendars automatically.

Integration with Blackboard/Canvas so students see events tied to their courses or orgs.

Campus ID integration: scan your student ID (or app QR code) for event entry + attendance tracking.

👉 The difference between MVP and this list is stickiness. MVP helps students find events. These advanced features make them stay engaged daily and create a community hub, not just a calendar.

Got it ✅ — here’s a phased roadmap that shows how you can roll out your campus events app without overwhelming yourself or the users.

🚀 Phase 1: MVP (3–4 months)

Goal: Launch fast, solve the core problem — “What’s happening on campus?”

Event Listings (time, date, location, description)

Basic Filters & Search (by type or day/week)

RSVP + Calendar integration (Google/Apple/Outlook)

Authentication with student email (.edu)

Mobile-first web app design

⚡ Phase 2: Engagement Features (5–8 months)

Goal: Keep students coming back and make orgs want to post events here.

Push Notifications & Reminders for RSVP’d events

Club/Org Pages with their event feeds

Interactive Map with event pins

“Free Food Alert” tag

Check-in via QR code for attendance

Basic Analytics for clubs (views/RSVPs)

🌱 Phase 3: Social & Personalization (9–12 months)

Goal: Transform from a utility → into a social hub.

Personalized Feed (recommendations based on major/interests)

Friends Feature (see events friends RSVP’d to)

Event Group Chats (temporary chat rooms per event)

Gamification (badges, points, rewards for attending events)

“Happening Now” live event feed

🌟 Phase 4: Standout & Differentiation (1–2 years)

Goal: Make your app the go-to for all things campus life.

Career & Academic Tie-ins (career fairs, research talks, tutoring sessions)

Alumni/Volunteer/Community event integration

Advanced Map Features (AR navigation, accessibility routes, food heat maps)

Local Business Partnerships (discounts/freebies for attendees)

Loyalty Rewards Program (redeem points for campus perks)

🏆 Phase 5: Expansion & Scale (2+ years)

Goal: Expand beyond one campus and build a network effect.

Multi-campus support (students can switch campuses)

Campus-wide trending feed (“Top 5 events this week”)

Integration with Student Life Systems (Canvas/Blackboard, Campus ID scanning)

Monetization (sponsored events, premium features for orgs)

White-label version for universities to license

👉 This roadmap keeps you lean in the beginning, but gives you room to grow into a sticky, community-driven app that could eventually scale to multiple universities.
